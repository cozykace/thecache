# THE CACHE — features

A living, product-facing list of what THE CACHE does — kept current as we ship, ready for when this goes live to other people.

**The promise:** have a good time facing the skeletons in your closet. THE CACHE is a **free**, calm, playful, provably-private **life OS** — one secure home for the parts of your life you want to track, built from real research to work for brains that struggle with executive function (ADHD, autism, brain injury — and honestly, anyone overwhelmed). Your executive energy varies day to day; that's not a flaw. Cache exists to show you your own pattern and help you plan around it, instead of fighting it. Money is the first area of life it masters — because money has the highest stakes — and the same home is growing to hold your health, tasks, meals, and more.

_Last updated: 2026-07-13_

## Log your life in one tap
- **Daily check-in** — a full-screen, phone-first flow of big friendly buttons: one question per screen, confetti and EXP when you finish. Logging once feeds your whole cache.
- **Build your own deck** — choose the questions, the answer types, and where each answer lands (Money, Health, a custom Tracker, or the Day-log); drag to reorder. A plain-language line explains every choice.
- **The energy question** — "How's your energy right now?" on a one-tap 1–5 scale, from Drained to Charged. No wrong answer — Cache learns your pattern so you can plan around it.
- **Your energy pattern, visible** — the Energy widget turns every answer into one bar per day across the last two weeks, with today's energy up top and your average below. It updates live the moment a check-in finishes. A missed day shows as a quiet dash — information, never a failure.

## Build your life as a base
- **The Base** — flip from the widget board to a full-screen city view of your life: place, name, and color-code buildings for your income, expenses, and home; drive around; merge related buildings into districts.
- **Home Base** — the centerpiece: the people and pets in your household, each with their real costs, flowing into what you actually need to earn.
- **Auto-fill from cache** — build your starting city from your real numbers in one tap.
- **Two lenses, one truth** — every building matches a widget on the board; both read the same live data.

## Connect your money
- **Automatic bank sync** — link real accounts (read-only) and pull balances + transactions on demand.
- **Import statements** — drag a CSV onto the dashboard for cards that don't auto-connect, or to backfill older history. Duplicates are skipped automatically.
- **Local & private by design** — your financial data lives on your machine, never on a server, never committed anywhere.

## See where you stand
- **Total balance** with a per-account breakdown.
- **Where it's going** — spending grouped by category, with the real date range and a daily/monthly pace.
- **Months** — browse every past month (income vs. spending, net, and a category breakdown), built from your full transaction history.
- **Money flow** — a visual map of every account with checking up top cascading down to savings and cards.
- **Recurring radar** — flags charges that are **new**, **changed** (price moved >10%), or **stopped**, so a sneaky price hike or a forgotten subscription never slips by.
- **Clean merchant names** — raw bank gibberish is auto-tidied to readable names everywhere; your own renames always win.

## Understand your income
- **Auto-detected income** with gig/payroll smarts, and **define income your way** — tag which deposits actually count; your call sticks.
- **Link income to your work** — connect an income source to the Toggl project that earns it, so pay lines up with effort.

## Plan and decide
- **Safe-to-spend** forecast from your real daily burn, minus a reserve you choose.
- **The gap** — what you have versus what you need versus what you must earn to break even.
- **Work planner** — how many hours of gig work would close the gap.
- **Core vs. flex** — your non-negotiable spending versus what you could cut.
- **Subscriptions** — every recurring charge in one place, each markable core or flex.

## Make it yours
- **Three modes** — **Minimalist** keeps it simple, **Standard** is the balanced view, **Legendary** shows every button. Plain names, your choice, changeable any time.
- **Drag-and-drop board** — place, resize, and arrange widgets freely, with magnet snap, zoom, pan, and up to six independent pages.
- **Accessibility Hub** — a first-class home for comfort: one-tap presets, seizure-safe motion, adjustable contrast and text size, a color-blind-safe palette, full keyboard and screen-reader support. Accessibility requests jump the line.
- **Themes, fonts, icon stickers, custom cursor, optional soundtrack** — small touches that make it feel like yours.
- **Works on your phone** — on a small screen the board becomes a clean single-column scroll, and the check-in was born phone-first (privately, over your own network — never the public internet).

## Stay on top of it
- **Status & next actions** — one indicator surfaces the single most important thing to do next; click to see the full list.
- **Your cache is a character** — it levels up as you do the real work: EXP, merit badges, and a journey earned by living, never bought.
- **Roadmap in the app** — see what's shipped and what's next, right from the dock.
- **Report a bug or request** — a built-in feedback button; your note goes straight to the maker and into the beta feedback inbox, no email app required.

## Your data, owned
- **Permanent ledger** — nothing is lost once it's synced; every number provably derives from it.
- **Daily local backups** and a live **Private & verified** integrity check.
- **Plain files you own** — your data is simple JSON on your disk. No lock-in, ever.
- **Encrypted backups & Cache cloud (beta)** — seal everything with a passphrase only you know; sync it end-to-end encrypted and open your cache anywhere. The server only ever stores an unreadable blob. Local is free forever; the cloud is opt-in.

## What's coming
The roadmap tab (right in the app) shows what's next: check-ins that sync across your devices, a coached setup for first-time users, a work/rest timer, tasks that reward showing up, meals, and the rest of the twelve areas of life — one honest brick at a time.
