## 🏙️ The Base — your life as a city-builder (NORTH STAR, 2026-06-27)
The big reframe: the main view becomes a **base/city-builder game of your real life**, not the widget board. Why it works — the best base-builders (Frostpunk, Cities: Skylines, StarCraft) are *fun to manage* because they're **coherent**: what you make, you can spend; what you plan actually happens. Real-life money feels unplayable because it's incoherent. THE CACHE makes your life as coherent, reliable, and trustable as a game board — so paying attention to it is both **real and fun**, and every minute spent is worthwhile. The widget board stays as an alternate view; **each building in the base is the same as a widget in the board** (two lenses, one truth).
- [~] **🏠 Home Base — the centerpiece** (STARTED 2026-06-27) — the first thing you place and the foundation of the whole game: it holds your resources and everything builds off it. You configure it to match your real life — the people in your household, pets, each with their real needs and **costs** — and those flow *coherently* through the rest of the game (add a pet's monthly cost → it shows up in your upkeep → in "how much you need by when"). Built on the Money Map logic (income streams; fixed vs. flexible expenses; daily/weekly/monthly/yearly charges → how much you really need by what date). Full-screen, gamified, skinnable. **v1 BUILT 2026-06-27:** the 🏙️ **base** pill opens a full-screen Base view — an isometric ground with the Home Base at center; tap it to open a household sheet (add people/pets + monthly cost each) with live totals (real monthly income from your cache − household upkeep = left over). Art loads from the **skins pipeline** (`skins/default/`, inline fallback). Persists to `money.homebase`. Pending Cozy's eyeball; next: fixed-vs-flex expenses, a "needed-by" date, more buildings.
- [ ] **🏗️ Buildings + streets = your money flow, as a city** (BIG, the core of the base view) — the base becomes a coherent map of your finances: place **buildings** of different kinds (a **widget-building** like Home Base; **income-source** buildings; **expense** buildings; a **group-of-expenses** district), each **titleable** and color-coded by type. Then draw **streets between them with one-way arrows** showing how resources flow (income → Home Base → expenses), so your whole money map is something you literally build and walk. This is the Money Flow widget reborn as a city. Bricks: a building model (id, name, type, x/y, links), place/move buildings on the grid, a street/edge tool with directional arrows, and coherence so a building's real numbers drive the flow. Requested 2026-06-27. (v1 groundwork shipped: Home Base is now titleable + a simplified iso building on the flat grid.)
- [ ] **🍲 The Pantry — meal planning that respects your money** — a building that closes the loop most budgets miss: plan meals ahead, track the grocery spend, see the real **cost per meal**, whether it actually beats eating out, and whether you used everything up so the money you spent is respected. Turns "I keep overspending on takeout" into a coherent, playable routine. A top-priority building. (Renamed from 'Larder' to 'Pantry' 2026-07-12 — plain literal language per PRINCIPLES.)
- [ ] **🎨 Skins system (art + sound) + creator SOP** — every building/UI/sound is a swappable **skin**. A clear folder-structure + naming convention (`skins/<name>/…` + a manifest) so anyone can make and share their own art/sound skin; the built-in art ships as the default skin with every piece visible in folders as inspiration. Placeholder art is fine now — the pipeline is what we're building. See `SKINS.md`.
- [ ] **🧭 Buildings ⇆ widgets coherence** — every structure in the base maps 1:1 to a widget in the board view, so people who prefer the dashboard and people who prefer the city both get the same data, their way.
- [ ] **🛠️ Cache Dev Visualizer (founder view)** — a full-screen, 100% visualized map of how THE CACHE itself is built: the data pipeline from the banks, which widgets touch which data in the cache, what language each piece is written in and when it was last updated. Unlocked inside a founder account. Evolves the Dev Tree widget.
- [ ] **🗺️ Customizable life-area regions** — carve your base into the areas that matter to *you* (creative, health, financial, and more), so no two people's cache tracks the same things.
- [ ] **🌱 Living regions that grow with you** — each area of your base gets visibly richer and more developed as you build real history there, so progress is something you can actually see.
- [x] **🧱 Merge buildings** (2026-07-10) — combine related buildings (income streams, expenses) into one bigger structure so a busy area reads as one clear shape; drag to reposition, un-merge to split any source back out.
- [ ] **✈️ The flight arc — Ground City → Cloud City** (NORTH-STAR STORY, 2026-07-16) — the game's narrative spine: everyone wants to get their life off the ground and feel like they're flying. You start with your **boots on the ground** — the city-builder on solid earth, because first you get grounded in your real data and get your basic support running. Then you define what **flying** means for *you* (you define your own success, and check in on that definition as life changes), and the cache maps the runway — what it takes, how long, how to spread it out. When you're actually flying, the game unlocks the **Cloud City**: a second build site in the sky for your dreams, connected to your ground city by a permanent pipeline — because the dreams are always fed by the foundation. And the story works both ways: when you've been stretched too thin too long, the cache can tell you it's time to **come home and get grounded** — and when your ground city is solid, it can tell you you're clear to fly. One story, many shapes: a traveling artist's cache and a parent-of-two's cache both fit.
- [ ] **🎭 Built through real archetypes** (design protocol, 2026-07-16) — every feature gets designed and double-checked through the eyes of a roster of real kinds of people, so the cache feels at home no matter the shape of your life: the many-projects creative gig-worker, the paper-first artist who wants minimal screen time, the busy parent managing a whole household, the kid (always under a parent's account, ~10 minutes a day max, then back to real life), the young adult finding their people, the traveler whose home base holds while they roam. The roster sharpens over time against what real users actually need — and the first rule stays the first rule: anything the cache can figure out from your data, it figures out, so *you* don't spend more time in an app.
