# THE CACHE — features

A living, product-facing list of what THE CACHE does — kept current as we ship, ready for when this goes live to other people.

**The promise:** have a good time facing the skeletons in your closet. THE CACHE is a **free**, calm, playful, provably-private **life OS** — one secure home for the parts of your life you want to track, built from real research to work for brains that struggle with executive function (ADHD, autism, brain injury — and honestly, anyone overwhelmed). Your executive energy varies day to day; that's not a flaw. Cache exists to show you your own pattern and help you plan around it, instead of fighting it. Money is the first area of life it masters — because money has the highest stakes — and the same home is growing to hold your health, tasks, meals, and more.

_Last updated: 2026-07-25_

## Log your life in one tap
- **🃏 The deck** — the one button to remember. Always in the same spot on every device, it opens your day one card at a time: big friendly buttons, one question per screen, confetti and EXP when you finish. One minute keeps your whole cache fed — and a one-time coaching card teaches the habit the first day.
- **Build your own deck** — choose the questions, the answer types, and where each answer lands (Money, Health, a custom Tracker, or the Day-log); drag to reorder. A plain-language line explains every choice.
- **Tasks & subtasks** — add the things you need to do and remember, break any of them into subtasks as deep as you like, and check them off (a little EXP each time). Delete a whole branch and undo it with one tap. Every task syncs on its own, so two devices editing different parts both keep their work.
- **Turn a task into a habit** — one tap makes a task something you track instead of finish: a simple yes/no you tick each day, a number you log (minutes, reps, glasses of water), a **1–5 rating**, or **a few words** about how it went. It resets clean every morning on its own, and your history keeps every day's answer — never a checkbox you have to remember to un-tick.
- **Habits on your schedule** — a habit can repeat on the days *you* choose: every day, certain weekdays, monthly, yearly, or every-N, with a start date and a pause switch. On an off day it just says "not today" — dimmed and calm, never a red mark, and still tappable if you do it anyway as a bonus. Scheduled habits land on your calendar on their due days, where yes/no habits check off in place.
- **Projects — group the work, watch it move** — for a bigger effort, add the **Projects** widget: name a project, open it, and add the tasks it takes. Check them off and its progress bar fills. A project's tasks live inside it — not scattered through your loose Tasks list — and everything syncs task by task, so a phone edit and a laptop edit both survive. *(v1: task-count progress; due dates, habits, and multi-area projects are coming.)*
- **Routines on your schedule** — group a few tasks into a routine you pull up daily, weekly (pick the days), monthly, or yearly. Its card shows today's progress at a glance (or "not today" when it's off-schedule), and every step resets fresh each day the routine comes due. Open the routine to build it out right there — add steps, rename them, reorder with ↑/↓, or remove one — no hunting for the right ＋.
- **Scroll through your days** — the deck opens on today, but a smooth day-wheel at the bottom lets you slide to yesterday, tomorrow, or any recent day. Checking a habit off saves to the day you're looking at, so your history stays honest — forgot to log yesterday? Scroll back and tick it. A day that isn't today is clearly greyed (still fully editable), and one tap returns you to today. Each device remembers its own place.
- **The energy question** — "How's your energy right now?" on a one-tap 1–5 scale, from Drained to Charged. No wrong answer — Cache learns your pattern so you can plan around it.
- **Your energy pattern, visible** — the Energy widget turns every answer into one bar per day across the last two weeks, with today's energy up top and your average below. It updates live the moment a check-in finishes. A missed day shows as a quiet dash — information, never a failure.
- **Work / rest timer** — work a block, rest a block, with a longer rest every few blocks. The visible countdown does the time-keeping so your head doesn't have to, and gentle chimes mark the turns. All four numbers are yours to change, and pausing, skipping, or ending early is always one tap and never punished. Finishing a work block earns EXP.

## See your whole month
- **📅 A calendar built in** — a full-screen calendar, one tap from anywhere, that already knows your life: your tasks land on their due dates, your routines show on the days they run, and your events sit right alongside them. Nothing to copy over from another app — if it has a date in Cache, it's on the calendar. Switch between month, week, and a single-day agenda.
- **Real events, made simple** — add an event to any day in seconds: name it, timed or all-day, a start and an optional end for things that span days, and where in your life it belongs. Make it repeat — every day, certain weekdays, monthly, or yearly — with an optional stop date. It spreads itself across the calendar for you.
- **Do it from the calendar** — tap a day to see everything on it, check a task off without leaving, or tap anything to edit it. Your default view, whether the week starts Sunday or Monday, and how busy the month grid looks are all yours to set — and they follow you to every device.
- **Calm on a phone** — on a small screen the month softens to quiet dots so nothing overflows; tap a day to open its full list. Big tap targets, no clutter.

## Build your life as a base
- **The Base** — flip from the widget board to a full-screen city view of your life: place, name, and color-code buildings for your income, expenses, and home; drive around; merge related buildings into districts.
- **Home Base** — the centerpiece: the people and pets in your household, each with their real costs, flowing into what you actually need to earn.
- **Auto-fill from cache** — build your starting city from your real numbers in one tap.
- **Two lenses, one truth** — every building matches a widget on the board; both read the same live data.

## Connect your money
- **Automatic bank sync** — link real accounts (read-only) and pull balances + transactions on demand.
- **Connect your bank from the web — no computer needed** — paste your SimpleFIN token on your phone or laptop and your cache pulls your balances and transactions itself, computing everything in your browser and sealing it to your encrypted cache. SimpleFIN holds the bank login so The Cache never sees it; the key stays on your device so we can't read your money. A built-in "How does this work?" explains it plainly, you pull whenever you want (with a clear "these numbers are from…"), and a failed pull can never overwrite your good data.
- **Import a bank CSV right in your browser** — no desktop needed. Drop a CSV export onto your cache and it parses, builds your ledger, and computes your spending, safe-to-spend and income entirely on your device, then syncs it to your other devices — sealed and unreadable to the cloud. Perfect for cards that don't auto-connect, for backfilling older history, or for running your whole cache from the web alone.
- **Never double-counts** — re-import an overlapping statement as often as you like; duplicates are skipped, and anything you deleted stays deleted.
- **Local & private by design** — your financial data lives on your machine, never on a server, never committed anywhere.

## See where you stand
- **Total balance** with a per-account breakdown.
- **Where it's going** — spending grouped by category, with the real date range and a daily/monthly pace.
- **Months** — browse every past month (income vs. spending, net, and a category breakdown), built from your full transaction history.
- **Money flow** — a visual map of every account with checking up top cascading down to savings and cards.
- **Recurring radar** — flags charges that are **new**, **changed** (price moved >10%), or **stopped**, so a sneaky price hike or a forgotten subscription never slips by.
- **Clean merchant names** — raw bank gibberish is auto-tidied to readable names everywhere; your own renames always win.

## Understand your income
- **Auto-detected income** with gig/payroll smarts, and **define income your way** — tag which deposits actually count; your call sticks.
- **Link income to your work** — connect an income source to the Toggl project that earns it, so pay lines up with effort.

## Plan and decide
- **Safe-to-spend** forecast from your real daily burn, minus a reserve you choose.
- **The gap** — what you have versus what you need versus what you must earn to break even.
- **Work planner** — how many hours of gig work would close the gap.
- **Core vs. flex** — your non-negotiable spending versus what you could cut.
- **Subscriptions** — every recurring charge in one place, each markable core or flex.

## Make it yours
- **Three modes** — **Minimalist** keeps it simple, **Standard** is the balanced view, **Legendary** shows every button. Plain names, your choice, changeable any time.
- **Drag-and-drop board** — place, resize, and arrange widgets freely, with magnet snap, zoom, pan, and up to six independent pages.
- **Accessibility Hub** — a first-class home for comfort: one-tap presets, seizure-safe motion, adjustable contrast and text size, a color-blind-safe palette, full keyboard and screen-reader support. Accessibility requests jump the line.
- **Themes, fonts, icon stickers, custom cursor, optional soundtrack** — small touches that make it feel like yours.
- **A profile that's yours to edit** — tap your character card and the cache opens as your full profile: rename your cache, say who you are (name, pronouns, what you do, a private "about"), and see the level, badges, and journey you've earned. Everything autosaves as you type and stays private in your encrypted cache. If you've claimed an @handle, showing a display name next to it is one clearly-labeled switch — flip it off and it's gone from public view immediately.
- **Works on your phone** — on a small screen the board becomes a clean single-column scroll, and the check-in was born phone-first (privately, over your own network — never the public internet).

## Stay on top of it
- **One dot for all your connections** — tap the little cloud dot at the bottom of the board and one calm panel tells you, in plain words, whether your lifelines are healthy: **cloud sync** (synced ✓ / syncing / needs you, with when it last synced), your **bank**, and — on the desktop — the local **cache server**. Each has a colour-and-shape light, so a green dot means good and an open ring or square means it wants you, readable at a glance even in the black-and-white theme or if you're colour-blind. **Sync now** and your account controls (cloud settings, switch account, log out) live right there. It even works before you sign in, so you can still see your bank and server and find your way to cloud sign-in. (This replaced the old always-on green "synced" button — quieter board, and obvious where to look when something needs you.)
- **Status & next actions** — one indicator surfaces the single most important thing to do next; click to see the full list.
- **Your cache is a character** — it levels up as you do the real work: EXP, merit badges, and a journey earned by living, never bought.
- **Roadmap in the app** — see what's shipped and what's next, right from the dock.
- **What's new, quietly** — every update announces itself in one calm place: the 🔔 inside Messages. The messages button tells you at a glance who's waiting — top corner means a person, bottom corner means news — and neither ever flashes or interrupts. A note marks itself read after you've genuinely seen it for a few seconds (never while the tab is in the background), you can mark anything unread to come back later, and read state follows you across your devices. A fresh install starts clean — you only see news that lands after you arrive.
- **Report a bug or request** — a built-in feedback button; your note goes straight to the maker and into the beta feedback inbox, no email app required.
- **Your reports come back to you** — tick "Credit this to my cache" when you send a report (optional — anonymous is always welcome) and the loop actually closes: when your report gets fixed, a calm card tells you — your own words back to you, plus what changed — you earn EXP for it, and a **Cache builder** stat starts counting the reports of yours that made the app better for everyone. It appears with your first one; there's never an empty version shaming you.
- **A public status page** — thecache.app/status checks the app, the cloud, and the demo live from your own browser, shows the honest history of incidents and fixes, and gives you a report box that works even signed-out (with an email fallback if the cloud itself is down).

## Your data, owned
- **Permanent ledger** — nothing is lost once it's synced; every number provably derives from it.
- **Daily local backups** and a live **Private & verified** integrity check.
- **Plain files you own** — your data is simple JSON on your disk. No lock-in, ever.
- **Cache cloud (beta) — sign in and your cache follows you** — your data is sealed on your device before it leaves, and once you're set up it syncs itself: change something, it's in the cloud moments later; open the app anywhere, the latest is waiting. The cloud dot always tells you the truth (synced ✓ / syncing / needs you) — tap it for the full connection panel. Local is free forever; the cloud is opt-in.
- **Your key, your choice — zero-knowledge by default** — when you set up the cloud you pick, in plain words: **Just me** (only you can open your cache — we physically can't; the recommended default) or **Keep a spare with The Cache** (we hold a key so we can help you back in — which means we could technically read your data). No wrong answer, no crypto jargon. Either way a **recovery file** downloads during setup so a forgotten passphrase never means "gone," and you can add a one-time **recovery code** for paper or a password manager. Your cache has one key; each way in is its own sealed wrapping of it, so **any one opens your cache and none weakens the others**. A clear list in Settings lets you add or remove a method any time, and turning the spare key off genuinely removes it from our side.
- **No old account left without a net — on any device** — if your cache predates recovery files, it used to have none: your passphrase *was* the key, so forgetting it meant losing everything. Now the next time you open that cache with your passphrase — in the desktop app **or right in your browser** — it's quietly upgraded and a **recovery file** is handed to you with a single tap, alongside a calm heads-up (never a scary popup). The same net also reaches any account that somehow never got a recovery file. It stays zero-knowledge (only you can open it), your passphrase keeps working exactly as before, it only ever appears once (never nagging you), and if anything hiccups the upgrade just doesn't happen — your cache is never left half-changed. You don't do anything special; simply opening your cache is enough.
- **Forgot your password? Reset it yourself** — a quiet **Forgot password?** link under the sign-in fields emails you a reset link, on the web and in Settings. It always says the same calm thing whether or not that email has an account, so it can't be used to find out who's registered — and it's honest about the two things that aren't the same: a reset gets you back into your **account**, but if your cache is sealed **just for you** (zero-knowledge), your data still opens only with your **recovery file or code**. After you log back in the app tells you exactly that and points you to recovery, instead of pretending your data came back on its own. Accounts that keep a spare key with us just open as normal. The reset and verify links land on **The Cache's own page** — a clean "set a new password" / "you're verified" screen, not a bare hosting page — and the one-time code never shows up in a web address or a log, and is wiped from the bar the moment it's used.
- **One character, every device** — your EXP is a ledger: points earned on your phone, your desktop, offline on a plane, or even on an accidental fresh start all **add up** into the same character. Nothing you earn is ever erased by syncing — in any order, ever.
- **Your deck syncs question-by-question** — edit a card on your laptop while reordering one on your phone and both survive; a question you delete stays deleted; and a fresh device can never steamroll the deck you built.
- **A delete that sticks, and un-sticks** — removing a duplicate transaction holds across every device and survives a restore (nothing can quietly hand it back), and the Review panel keeps a Deleted list so a mistake is one tap from being undone — on every device.
- **Restore from cloud merges, never erases** — pulling your cloud copy onto a device combines your check-ins and permanent ledger (an older copy can never shrink your history), reconciles your tags, and merges your character and settings in — anything you did on this device since the last sync survives, and a full backup is taken first.
- **Widgets that tell the truth and heal themselves** — a backend blip never fakes an empty board: widgets keep the numbers they have, honest "backend off" notes replace fake empty states, saves that can't land say so and retry themselves the moment the server is back, and nothing you've saved can be wiped by a save made from a stale copy.
- **A sync checkmark that never lies** — the "cloud ✓" only shows when the cloud provably holds everything this device authored. If two devices save at nearly the same moment, the one whose changes got knocked out notices and quietly re-uploads them — no silent loss behind a green light.
- **Your tags follow you between computers** — categorizations, income tags, subscription renames, and deposit-to-project links merge tag-by-tag across your machines: each keeps every tag either has, a machine that's behind can't wipe a tag you just set, and a genuine re-tag resolves to the most recent one.
- **Every device keeps its own edits** — sync reconciles setting-by-setting, not last-one-wins: a real change on one device always beats a stale copy on another, badges and streak-marks combine instead of clobbering, and your journey's founding date can only ever move earlier. A phone that hasn't caught up can never silently undo something you just set on your desktop — and the per-device stuff (zoom, sidebar width, panel positions) stays where you put it on each device.
- **Encrypted file & WebDAV backups** — seal everything with your passphrase into a `.cache` file you own, or push it to your own WebDAV server. Those bundles open anywhere with just the passphrase.
- **A security promise you can actually read** — a plain-language `SECURITY.md` lays out exactly how your data is protected and where the line is: the encryption is open on purpose (secrecy that depends on hidden code isn't security), and it says straight out that only passphrase mode is a true "not even we can read it" guarantee. No jargon, no overselling — plus a clear, private way to report anything you find.
